the 797 audio factory in china builds condenser mic capsules for many pro audio companies
according to a forum post _“Their biggest partners ... Blue Microphones, Audio Technica, Parts Express, Rad Distribution (Audified, Avantone Pro, Behringer, Black Box Analog Design, Black Lion Audio, Bugera, FreqPort, Golden Age Audio, HEDD Audio, Heritage Audio, IsoVox, Kahayan Audio, Kark Teknik, Lab.Gruppen, Lindell Audio, Midas, Psychic Modulation, Tannoy, TC Electronic, TC Helicon, TK Audio, Turbosound, and United Studio Technologies) and also Warm Audio”_

apparently 797 was originally set up in the early 50's with assistance from Neumann of East Germany.
